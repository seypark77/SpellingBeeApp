# Privacy Policy for BeeReady: Spelling Bee Study

**Effective Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 14, 2026

Welcome to **BeeReady: Spelling Bee Study** ("BeeReady", "we", "our", or "us"). We are dedicated to providing a safe, educational, and fun learning environment for children, students, parents, and educators.

We take the privacy of our young learners very seriously. This Privacy Policy explains our commitment to protecting children's privacy in strict accordance with the **Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)**, the **Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA)**, Google Play's **Families Policy**, and Apple's **App Store Review Guidelines for Kids Category Apps**.

---

### 1. Summary of Our Key Privacy Principles

- 🚫 **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (such as full names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses).
- 📱 **100% On-Device Local Storage:** All student profiles, study lists, practice history, and reward points (Honey Points) are stored exclusively on your device.
- 🎙️ **Microphone & Speech Recognition:** Microphone access is used solely in real time for children to spell words out loud. Voice audio is processed live and is **never** recorded, stored, uploaded, or transmitted to any remote server.
- 🚫 **No Third-Party Advertising:** BeeReady is completely ad-free. We do not integrate any third-party advertising SDKs or tracking pixels.
- 🚫 **No Analytics or Behavior Tracking:** We do not track or profile user behavior across other apps or websites.
- 🍯 **No Real-Money Purchases:** The in-app "Wardrobe & Shop" uses strictly virtual honey points earned by practicing spelling words. There are no real-money transactions or microtransactions.

---

### 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not request, collect, or store any of the following:
- Personal identification details (e.g., child's real name, age, birth date, gender)
- Contact information (e.g., email address, home address, phone number)
- Account credentials (there is no account creation, login, or social network integration)
- Geolocation data (GPS or IP-based location)
- Device identifiers or advertising IDs (IDFA, GAID)
- Audio recordings (microphone input is processed live on-device and instantly discarded)

---

### 3. Local Data Stored On Your Device

To personalize the educational experience, BeeReady stores the following data locally on the user's device via local storage:
- **Local Speller Nickname & Avatar:** An optional nickname (e.g., "Speller #1") and chosen cartoon avatar.
- **Learning Progress & Word Mastery:** Words practiced, mastery levels, accuracy scores, and review bookmarks.
- **Achievements & Badges:** Unlocked spelling badges, streaks, and cumulative virtual Honey Points.
- **Customization Settings:** Selected cartoon outfits, hats, and accessories.

*This data never leaves your device. You can completely erase all data at any time by deleting the profile inside the app or uninstalling the app.*

---

### 4. Device Permissions Explained

- **Microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO` / `NSMicrophoneUsageDescription`):**
  - **Purpose:** Used strictly for the optional "Voice Speller" practice mode, allowing students to speak letters aloud (e.g., "B - E - E").
  - **Handling:** Audio input is converted to text in real time using your device's built-in speech recognition framework. Audio streams are immediately discarded and are never recorded or sent to any server.

---

### 5. Third-Party Services & Links

- **No Third-Party SDKs:** We do not include any third-party marketing, analytics, or behavioral advertising SDKs.
- **External Links:** In full compliance with Apple’s Kids Category and Google Play Families requirements, external web access is restricted to ensure children remain within a safe, distraction-free environment.

---

### 6. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) Compliance

BeeReady complies fully with COPPA regulations:
- We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.
- Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, parents and guardians can rest assured that their child's digital footprint remains 100% private.
- Parents who have questions about our privacy practices can contact us at any time using the contact details below.
