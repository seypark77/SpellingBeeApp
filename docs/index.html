Privacy Policy & Children's Online Privacy Protection Commitment

Effective Date: November 14, 2026 Target Audience: Students & Children (Ages 5–13)
Core Privacy Guarantee: BeeReady does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information from children or adults. All spelling progress, profiles, and practice data are kept strictly on your local device.

1. Summary of Our Key Principles

  • 🚫 Zero Personal Data Collection: No real names, emails, phone numbers, or account registrations.
  • 📱 100% On-Device Storage: All student lists, quiz scores, and reward Honey Points live locally on your phone or tablet.
  • 🎙️ Private Speech Recognition: Microphone access is only used live for the Voice Speller feature. Audio is processed on-device and is never recorded, saved, or uploaded to any server.
  • 🚫 Completely Ad-Free: No third-party advertisements, tracking pixels, or marketing analytics.
  • 🍯 Safe Virtual Rewards: The in-app Wardrobe & Shop uses earned virtual Honey Points. There are no real-money transactions or hidden fees.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

In accordance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and global privacy standards, we never ask for or collect:

  • Personal identification details (full names, age, birth dates)
  • Contact details (email addresses, phone numbers, physical locations)
  • Account credentials (there is no sign-in or social login)
  • Device advertising identifiers (IDFA, GAID)
  • Audio recordings or speech history

3. Local On-Device Data

BeeReady stores educational progress purely inside the device's local database (SQLite/local storage) to support your child's learning journey:

  • Local Nickname & Avatar: A student nickname (e.g., "Speller #1") and chosen cartoon bee character.
  • Spelling Mastery History: Words practiced, mastery levels, accuracy rates, and bookmarks.
  • Badges & Honey Points: Virtual achievement badges and virtual points earned through quiz practice.
  • Customization Items: Unlocked virtual hats, outfits, and props.

Parents and users can completely reset or erase this data at any time by deleting the profile inside the app or uninstalling the application.

4. Device Permissions

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO / NSMicrophoneUsageDescription): Used solely when a student uses the "Voice Speller" mode to spell letters aloud (e.g., "B - E - E"). Speech is processed live using on-device recognition and is immediately discarded.

5. Third-Party Services & Advertising

BeeReady contains zero third-party advertising and no marketing tracking frameworks. We believe children's learning environments should be completely free of commercial interruptions.

6. COPPA Compliance (Children Under 13)

We strictly comply with COPPA guidelines. Because BeeReady does not collect any personally identifiable information, children can safely practice spelling without any risk to their privacy. Parents with any questions or requests can reach us directly at any time.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may periodically update this policy to reflect new app features. Any updates will always be reflected on this page with a revised "Effective Date".
